# Claude Resume

# Make Your Resume Unrejectable — The 4 Skills

Four prompts that run your resume through the same gauntlet a real application faces: the software, the recruiter, the rewrite, and the interview. Run them **in order, in one Claude chat**, so each step builds on the last. Works on the free tier.

## Before you start

- Your current resume — paste the text or upload the file.
- The exact role and company you're targeting.
- 1–3 **real** job descriptions for that role (copy them from LinkedIn or the company site).
- One Claude chat. Keep all four prompts in the same chat so the context carries through.

---

## 1. The Diagnoser — read it like the software does

Most resumes die in an applicant tracking system before a human sees them. This finds what breaks the parse.

```
You are an applicant tracking system (ATS) resume parser. Here is my resume:

[paste your full resume]

Read it the way an ATS does — for machine-readability, not style. Then:
1. Flag anything that breaks parsing: tables, columns, text boxes, images, icons,
   headers/footers, unusual section titles, or non-standard fonts.
2. List every section an ATS might misread or drop, and why.
3. Give me the exact fix for each one, rewritten so a parser reads it cleanly.
Rank the issues from most to least likely to get me filtered out.
```

> ⚠️ Claude checks your resume against how ATS parsers generally work — it's a strong proxy, not one specific company's system. Treat each flag as "very likely a problem," not a guarantee.
> 

---

## 2. The Recruiter — find the keywords you're missing

A recruiter skims for the words that match the role. This tells you which ones you're missing before they do.

```
You are a senior recruiter hiring for this role: [role + company].

Here are real job descriptions for it:
[paste 1–3 job descriptions]

And here is my resume:
[paste your resume]

Do three things:
1. Pull the keywords and skills that show up across these postings — especially
   the ones that repeat.
2. Tell me which are missing or weak in my resume.
3. Give me the top 10 to add, ranked by how often they appeared and how much they
   matter for this role.
Only list skills I can honestly back up — flag any I'd need to actually learn.
```

> 💡 No postings handy? Turn on web search in Claude and add: *"Search for current job postings for this role and base your answer on those."* Pasting 1–3 real ones is still the sharpest input.
> 

---

## 3. The Rewriter — rebuild every bullet with Google's XYZ formula

This is the cheat code. It forces every line into a result, a number, and the method behind it.

```
Rewrite my experience section using the missing keywords from above (only the ones
I can back up) and Google's XYZ formula:

"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]."

Rules:
- Every bullet shows a result, a number, and the method.
- Work the keywords in naturally — no stuffing.
- Kill red flags and vague filler.
- If I'm missing a number for a bullet, ask me for it — do not invent one.
```

> ⚠️ Never let it make up metrics. If you don't have a number, give it the real one or keep the bullet honest — fake stats collapse the moment someone asks about them in the interview.
> 

---

## 4. The Hiring Manager — the interview before the interview

Now the rewritten resume meets the person who decides. This is your free reps.

```
Now act as the hiring manager for this exact role. Using my rewritten resume:
1. Interview me for real — one question at a time, starting easy and getting harder,
   including the toughest technical questions for this field.
2. Wait for my answer before the next question.
3. After each answer, score it out of 10 and tell me exactly what would make it a 10.
Run about 8 questions, then give me an overall hire / no-hire call and the 3 things
to fix before a real interview.
```

> 💡 Answer out loud, in real time, like the actual interview. The point is reps under pressure — not perfect typed answers.
> 

---

## What "done" looks like

- [ ]  Resume parses cleanly — no ATS red flags left
- [ ]  Every must-have keyword from the real postings is in (and true)
- [ ]  Every bullet has a result + a number + the method
- [ ]  You've run a full mock interview and know your 3 weakest answers

You just put yourself through the whole hiring process — before a single company could.
